FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is this a web app, or do I need to download it from Google Play or the App Store?

Both — there's a Progressive Web App you can open directly in your browser, plus the official app on Google Play and the App Store. You can also add it to your home screen so it behaves like an installed app.

Does it work without internet?

Yes — seniors can still see saved medical IDs and contacts even offline. Internet is only needed when syncing new data to the dashboard.

How many seniors can I manage on one account?

1 senior on the Free and Essential plans, up to 4 seniors on the Family Plan — all managed from a single dashboard.

How is my family's data protected?

We use row-level security in the database — every family has separate, protected access to only their own data. No other household can see anyone else's information.

Can I cancel or downgrade anytime?

Yes. There's no long-term contract — you can drop to the Free plan anytime from Settings in the dashboard.
