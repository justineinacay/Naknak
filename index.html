Frequently asked

Good questions deserve clear answers.

Is NakNak only for senior citizens and PWDs?

No. NakNak is designed for Filipino households of all ages and needs. Accessibility remains important, but the product is meant for anyone who wants better everyday care and preparedness.

Is NakNak Family already available for ₱199/month?

Not yet. ₱199/month and ₱1,990/year are proposed Philippine launch prices. Paid subscriptions and the full Family feature set are not active. Explore the current dashboard to see what is available today.

Can I use NakNak for free?

The Free plan is intended to remain free, including essential safety features. The existing web beta can be explored now; exact capabilities depend on the build you open.

Does SOS automatically alert relatives or emergency responders?

No. In the current native milestone, SOS activity is recorded locally and an SOS-opened status may be synchronized to a paired dashboard when online. NakNak does not yet automatically place calls, send SMS, or deliver remote push notifications.

Does NakNak work without internet?

Some core information and phone-side tools are designed to work locally, including stored contacts and medication schedules in the native app. Pairing and server-confirmed family status require a connection. Always test the particular app version and device before relying on any capability.

Is NakNak on the App Store or Google Play?

Not at this stage. The web beta is available to explore, while public native-app release requires further testing and store review.

Can I pay and download NakNak immediately?

Not yet. The intended Family flow is secure checkout followed by a verified download or approved store installation. No payment is accepted on this demonstration website. Android direct download requires a tested, signed release file; iOS distribution must use App Store or TestFlight as appropriate. Free web-beta access remains available without payment.

Will NakNak be available outside the Philippines?

The Philippines is our first focus. International expansion may follow once the product proves useful and can be localized responsibly for other countries.
