## Product positioning
NakNak is for Filipino families and households. It offers a deliberately simple safety home, local medicine reminders and optional family connection. Some users need larger buttons or accessibility settings; the product should not assume every user is elderly or disabled.

**Name:** NakNak
**Tagline:** Care that never sleeps.
**Suggested category:** Lifestyle / Utilities (confirm store category and applicable medical declarations with the actual release).
**Suggested short description:** Emergency contacts, check-ins and medication reminders for Filipino families.

## Suggested full store description (English)
NakNak helps Filipino families keep everyday care and safety actions close at hand.

From one simple home screen, family members can find their saved emergency contacts, open the phone dialer, record an Ayos Ako check-in, organize medication schedules and choose accessibility settings that suit them. Essential information remains on the phone for local use.

With optional Family Code pairing, a supported check-in or SOS-opened event can be reported to an authenticated household dashboard **when the server confirms it**. The Family Dashboard keeps the last confirmed record visible, so families can distinguish a locally saved event from a connected update.

NakNak is a support tool, not an emergency response service. Calling depends on your device and mobile network. NakNak does not currently provide automatic emergency calls, guaranteed caregiver push/SMS, continuous location tracking or fall detection.

**Note:** App Store/Google Play availability, connected check-ins and local reminders must be physically tested before the store description is published. Do not list unsupported beta or planned features as live.
